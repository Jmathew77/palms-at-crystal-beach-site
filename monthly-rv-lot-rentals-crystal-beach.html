FAQ

Common questions

How much is a monthly RV lot rental in Crystal Beach?

Monthly RV lot rentals start at $415 for standard non-concrete pads and $450 for premium concrete pads. Discounts are available for 6-month and 12-month stays.

Do you offer long-term RV stays?

Yes. The Palms specializes in monthly, long-term RV lot rentals on Bolivar Peninsula, with discounts for 6-month and 12-month commitments.

What is included with a monthly RV site?

Monthly sites include up to $125 of electricity each month, gated access, free high-speed Wi-Fi, onsite laundry, outdoor showers, and access to the fishing and crabbing pier.

Is electricity included in monthly RV lot rent?

Yes. Monthly RV lot rentals at The Palms include up to $125 of electricity usage each month, plus free high-speed Wi-Fi. If your electric usage goes over $125 in a month, only the overage is billed to your account.
