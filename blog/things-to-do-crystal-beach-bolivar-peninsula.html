Things to Do and Where to Eat in Crystal Beach and Bolivar Peninsula

By The Palms at Crystal Beach RV Park · November 14, 2026 · 6 min read

From the Gulf beach and Fort Travis to Stingaree, the Nauti Beaver Hut and live music on Highway 87, here is our guide to the top things to do and places to eat on Bolivar Peninsula.

Crystal Beach and the rest of Bolivar Peninsula are made for slow days: wide beaches, big skies, fishing, history, and a handful of local favorites for dinner and live music. This is our guide to the top things to do and where to eat, from the Gulf beach to Fort Travis, plus popular spots like the Nauti Beaver Hut (often searched as "Naughty Beaver") and Stingaree. We are not affiliated with the businesses below, and hours change, so call ahead.

Beaches and outdoors

Crystal Beach and the Bolivar Beach Pavilion

Crystal Beach's long stretch of open sand is the main event. The Bolivar Beach Pavilion offers parking, restrooms, rinse-off showers and water fountains, which makes it a handy beach stop. You can drive along the beach without a permit, but a vehicle parked on the sand needs a Bolivar Beach Parking Permit.

Fort Travis Seashore Park

In Port Bolivar, near the ferry landing, this park is built around a coastal-artillery fort from the late 1890s (sources give 1898 or 1899). You can explore concrete gun emplacements and bunkers, and the park has shaded picnic areas, wetland overlooks and a playground.

Bolivar Point Lighthouse

Built in 1872 and decommissioned in 1933, the lighthouse is a popular photo stop near Fort Travis. Visitors can't go inside, and it has been repainted in its original black-and-white stripes.

Birding at Bolivar Flats and beyond

The Bolivar Flats Shorebird Sanctuary is one of the best shorebird sites on the Gulf Coast, and Texas Parks and Wildlife's Bolivar Loop lists more stops. One local guide also describes the North Jetty and Big Reef Nature Park at Port Bolivar as a quiet corner for fishing and birding.

Fishing and crabbing

Surf, bay and pier fishing are all part of life here. Our fishing and crabbing guide covers what you can catch and the license and crab rules to check.

Gregory Park

Gregory Park in Crystal Beach, at 900 Barbados Dr., has a baseball field and a covered basketball court. It is also where the local Mardi Gras parade ends.

A free ferry day trip to Galveston

The TxDOT ferry costs nothing and crosses in about 18 minutes. On the other side you'll find museums, the Strand district and the beaches of Galveston Island. Towing your RV? See our Bolivar ferry guide.

A few tips: bring cash and patience on holiday weekends, call ahead if you're coming in the off-season, and plan a designated driver. The beach counts as a road, so drinking and driving laws apply there too. Quiet hours at The Palms begin at 10:30 p.m., so plan your return accordingly.

Annual events

  • Mardi Gras parade. The Crystal Beach parade runs along Highway 87 to Gregory Park in late winter (it was held February 10 in 2024 and March 1 in 2025).
  • Crab Festival and Stingaree Music Festival. These are among the big annual events. Dates change, so check before you plan.
  • Jeep Weekend. See our Jeep Weekend RV camping guide.
  • Fourth of July. See our July 4th guide.

A sample three-day itinerary

  • Day 1: Morning on the beach at the pavilion, lunch at the Nauti Beaver Hut or Tiki Beach, an afternoon on the fishing and crabbing pier, and a sunset dinner at Stingaree (arrive early).
  • Day 2: Fort Travis and the lighthouse, a stop at Bolivar Flats for the birds, then the ferry to Galveston for the afternoon.
  • Day 3: Fish or crab in the morning, grab tacos for lunch, and finish with live music on a Friday or Saturday night.

Frequently asked questions

What are the top things to do in Crystal Beach, TX?

Popular choices are the open Gulf beach and Bolivar Beach Pavilion, Fort Travis Seashore Park, the Bolivar Point Lighthouse, birding at Bolivar Flats, fishing and crabbing, and a free ferry day trip to Galveston.

What is the Nauti Beaver Hut?

The Nauti Beaver Hut, often searched as Naughty Beaver, is a bar and grill at 1709 Highway 87 in Crystal Beach. Its website lists burgers, tacos and fish and chips. Call ahead for current hours.

Where can I hear live music in Crystal Beach?

Local guides and tourism listings mention live music at venues on Highway 87 and Stingaree Drive, including Tiki Beach Bar & Grill, Jose's Cantina, HardHeads Icehouse & Grill and Stingaree. Music is most common on warm-weather and holiday weekends.

Where can I eat seafood in Crystal Beach?

Stingaree Restaurant & Marina is the most frequently recommended seafood spot, with waterfront dining on the bay side. Tiki Beach Bar & Grill and Tia Juanita's Fish Camp are also popular. Check hours before you go.

Is there a Crystal Beach beach access with restrooms and showers?

Yes. The Bolivar Beach Pavilion in Crystal Beach offers parking, restrooms, rinse-off showers and water fountains.
