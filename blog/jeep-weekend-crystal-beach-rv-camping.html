Jeep Weekend in Crystal Beach: RV Camping, Beach Rules and What to Expect

By The Palms at Crystal Beach RV Park · November 14, 2026 · 5 min read

Heading to Jeep Weekend on Bolivar Peninsula? Here are the dates, the 2026 beach rules, what to expect on the roads, and how to book an RV site at The Palms.

Every spring, Jeep owners from across Texas and beyond head to Bolivar Peninsula for Jeep Weekend, also known as Go Topless Weekend. It is one of the busiest weekends of the year in Crystal Beach, so knowing where you'll stay and what the rules are matters. This guide is based on 2026 reports and the rules at The Palms at Crystal Beach RV Park. Dates and rules change from year to year, so confirm the latest details with Galveston County before you go.

Jeep Weekend quick facts

  • 2026 dates: May 14–18 (in 2025 it was May 16–19)
  • 2027 dates: not announced as of November 2026
  • Event rate at The Palms: $85 per night. Call to confirm which dates it covers each year
  • Check-in / check-out: 2:00 p.m. / 12:00 noon
  • Book: 936-666-3423. Early reservations are recommended

Why you need a real campsite

For Jeep Weekend 2026, Galveston County said camping was prohibited on all Bolivar Peninsula beaches from May 14 to 18, and parking on the beach within the designated event areas was not allowed. That makes an RV park the practical place to stay. You get an assigned site, outdoor showers, laundry and a gate between your rig and the crowd.

What to expect from the 2026 rules

Reports on the 2026 plan described these rules. Treat them as a guide to what's likely, because the county announces its rules each year before the event. Check the Galveston County Jeep Weekend page for the current version.

  • Beach parking permit. A Bolivar beach parking permit (about $10) was required for beach access. It's sold at local businesses and the Bolivar Peninsula Chamber of Commerce.
  • One-way beach loop. Beach traffic ran one way, with limited entry and exit points and other beach access points closed.
  • Beach driving limits. A 15 mph speed limit, no ATVs, golf carts or UTVs, and no driving on the dunes.
  • No glass, no bonfires, no littering.
  • No camping or parking on the beach within event areas.
  • Heavy enforcement. The Galveston County Sheriff's Office ran a "zero tolerance" plan with a large law-enforcement presence.

Staying at The Palms for Jeep Weekend

  • Rate and booking. Our holiday and event rate is $85 a night. Prepaid amounts are non-refundable unless management agrees in writing, and that includes weather and ferry delays, so read the terms before you book.
  • Gated access. The park has managed, gated entry.
  • Quiet hours. They run 10:30 p.m. to 6:00 a.m., every night, and loud music and parties are not allowed. Think of The Palms as your base for sleeping and recharging after a day on the beach.
  • Getting around the park. The speed limit is 5 mph. Vehicle repairs and oil changes aren't allowed on the property. ATVs, minibikes and gas scooters are prohibited, and electric golf carts need a licensed driver and management approval.
  • Group size. A site is limited to 2 adults and 4 children, and extra tents or sleeping structures need written approval. For a bigger group, our 3 bed, 2 bath beach house is available through Airbnb.
  • Extra vehicles. Additional vehicle fees apply, so mention your Jeep or tow vehicle when you book.
  • Fires and fireworks. Open fires and fireworks are not allowed. BBQ grills and contained fire pits must be watched and fully put out after use.
  • Pets. Up to 2 per site, on a leash outside your RV.

Getting to Crystal Beach during the event

Officials warned of heavy congestion and traffic delays across the peninsula in 2026, with some roads restricted or redirected. Allow extra time. If you're coming by way of Galveston, expect a long wait at the free Bolivar ferry, which TxDOT says can top two hours in busy periods. Coming inland by way of I-10 and Highway 124 avoids the boat. Call us before you leave for arrival instructions, and arrive after 2:00 p.m. unless early check-in has been approved.

Jeep Weekend checklist

  • Beach parking permit on your windshield or as the county directs
  • Coolers and drinks in cans or plastic, never glass
  • Sunscreen, water and a plan for shade, since the beach has little
  • A tow strap and shovel if you're driving on soft sand, and know your Jeep's limits
  • Your confirmation and our number: 936-666-3423

Frequently asked questions

When is Jeep Weekend in Crystal Beach?

Jeep Weekend 2026 ran May 14–18, and in 2025 it was May 16–19. Dates for 2027 had not been announced as of November 2026. Check Galveston County and the Bolivar Peninsula Tourism and Visitors Center for updates.

Can I camp on the beach during Jeep Weekend?

In 2026, Galveston County prohibited camping on all Bolivar Peninsula beaches from May 14 to 18. An RV park is the practical place to stay.

Do I need a beach parking permit for Jeep Weekend?

Reports on the 2026 event said a Bolivar beach parking permit, about $10, was required for beach access. Permits are sold at local businesses and the Bolivar Peninsula Chamber of Commerce. Confirm current rules with the county.

How much is an RV site at The Palms during Jeep Weekend?

The holiday and event rate is $85 per night. Call 936-666-3423 to confirm the dates it covers and to check availability.

Does The Palms have quiet hours during Jeep Weekend?

Yes. Quiet hours are 10:30 p.m. to 6:00 a.m. every night, and loud music and parties are not allowed.
