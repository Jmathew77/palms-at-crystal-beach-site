Long-Term RV Living for Traveling Workers on the Upper Texas Coast

By The Palms at Crystal Beach RV Park · November 14, 2026 · 6 min read

Working a temporary assignment near Galveston? Here's how weekly and monthly RV lots compare with hotels and short-term leases, what's included in the rent, and the commute question to answer before you book.

If your work takes you to the upper Texas coast for a few weeks or months at a time, where you sleep matters. Traveling nurses, contract crews, inspectors, construction and project workers all face the same choice: hotel, short-term lease or your own RV. For many people an RV on a monthly lot is the most comfortable and the most affordable option. Here's how it works at The Palms at Crystal Beach RV Park on Bolivar Peninsula.

Why workers choose an RV lot

  • You bring your own home. Your bed, kitchen and routines travel with you from job to job.
  • No lease to break. Weekly and monthly stays fit assignments that can start, extend or end on short notice.
  • Lower housing costs. If your employer pays a housing or lodging stipend, a low lot rent lets you keep more of it, depending on how your arrangement is set up.

Four weekly stays add up to $800, which is $300 more than a month on a premium concrete pad. If your assignment will run past a few weeks, switching to monthly saves real money. Ask about our discounts for 6-month and 12-month stays on the monthly rentals page.

All bills paid, with free Wi-Fi

Monthly rent at The Palms is all bills paid. Water, sewer, trash and free high-speed Wi-Fi are included, along with up to $125 of electricity each month. You have no utility accounts to set up and fewer bills to track. Electric use above $125 in a month is billed to your account as an overage, so it pays to watch your A/C use in summer. What you get at The Palms

  • Premium concrete pads and standard pads, 37 sites in all
  • Gated access
  • Free high-speed Wi-Fi for work calls, paperwork and streaming
  • Onsite laundry and outdoor showers
  • A fishing and crabbing pier and a community area for time off

Plan your commute before you book

Crystal Beach is on Bolivar Peninsula, so there are two ways to reach the rest of Galveston County and Southeast Texas: the free Galveston–Port Bolivar Ferry, or Highway 124 inland to I-10 near Winnie. Ferry waits can be long on summer weekends and holidays, and the drive around the bay is much longer than the 18-minute crossing.

That means The Palms is best for workers whose job sites are on or near the peninsula, who can time their trips around the ferry, or who work remotely or on flexible schedules. Before booking, map the route from our address (1250 N Crystal Beach Road) to your job site at the times you'll actually be driving. If the commute works, you come home to a quiet beach town instead of a hotel parking lot.

Questions to ask before you arrive

  • Dates and rig length. Tell us how long you'll stay and how long your RV is so we can match you with the right pad.
  • Work vehicles. Additional vehicles are $30 a month each on monthly stays. Ask about parking for work trucks.
  • Deposit. We collect a $175 security deposit for stays of one week or longer.
  • RV age and condition. RVs should generally be 10 years old or newer unless management approves an exception, and must be self-contained and in good working order.
  • Mail and packages. They can't be received at the park without prior written management approval, so arrange forwarding or ask the office first.
  • Documentation. Stays are under a site use license agreement rather than a lease. If your employer needs receipts or a copy of the agreement, ask the office when you book.

Frequently asked questions

What are the weekly and monthly RV rates at The Palms?

Weekly RV sites are $200 per week. Monthly lots are $450 for a standard pad and $500 for a premium concrete pad, with discounts for 6-month and 12-month stays.

What is included in monthly RV lot rent?

Monthly rent is all bills paid: water, sewer, trash, free high-speed Wi-Fi and up to $125 of electricity each month are included. Electric use above $125 is billed as an overage.

Can I work remotely from The Palms?

Free high-speed Wi-Fi is included. It is shared among guests, so if you depend on it for important video calls, consider a backup cellular hotspot.

Is there a minimum stay or deposit?

A $175 security deposit applies to stays of one week or longer. Call 936-666-3423 to confirm current minimum stays and availability.

Can I park a work truck?

Additional vehicles are $30 per month each on monthly stays. Ask the office about parking for work trucks when you book.

How do I get from Crystal Beach to Galveston?

Take the free TxDOT ferry from Port Bolivar, about an 18-minute crossing, or drive inland by way of Highway 124 and I-10. Ferry waits can be long on summer weekends and holidays.
