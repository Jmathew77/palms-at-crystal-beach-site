Fourth of July in Crystal Beach: RV Camping, Fireworks and Beach Tips

By The Palms at Crystal Beach RV Park · November 14, 2026 · 5 min read

Planning July 4th on Bolivar Peninsula? Here's what to expect from the fireworks, the beach rules, the ferry and the park rules, plus how to book an RV site at The Palms.

The Fourth of July is one of the busiest weekends on Bolivar Peninsula. The beaches fill up, the sky fills with fireworks, and every site at The Palms at Crystal Beach RV Park is in demand. This guide covers what to expect, the beach rules, the ferry, and the park rules that matter most on a holiday weekend.

July 4th quick facts

  • Holiday rate at The Palms: $85 per night, from two days before July 4th through July 4th
  • Check-in / check-out: 2:00 p.m. / 12:00 noon
  • Fireworks: watch them on the beach, but they are prohibited inside the park
  • Quiet hours: 10:30 p.m. to 6:00 a.m.
  • Book early: 936-666-3423

Why spend July 4th in Crystal Beach

The Bolivar Peninsula Tourism and Visitors Center describes most of the peninsula's 27 miles of beachfront as one giant fireworks display on the Fourth. You can drive on the sand, set up near the water and watch the show up and down the coast. Local venues have also hosted live music on Fourth of July weekend in past years. Schedules change each summer, so check the visitors center and CrystalBeach.com for current events.

Fireworks: what's allowed

Fireworks are prohibited at The Palms. That's a park rule, and it includes anything you'd shoot off at home, so please leave them in the car or at home. Watching from the beach is the way to enjoy the show. If you do plan to buy fireworks locally, check Galveston County for any current restrictions or burn bans, which can apply in dry conditions, and clean up after yourself on the sand.

Beach rules for the holiday

  • Parking permit. You can drive along the beach without one, but a vehicle parked on the sand needs a Bolivar Beach Parking Permit.
  • No glass. Glass containers are not allowed on the beach.
  • Drinking. Adults of legal drinking age may drink on the beach, but the beach counts as a road. No open containers in vehicles, and no drinking and driving. Open-container laws apply to ATVs, golf carts and other vehicles too.
  • Be ready for crowds. Expect a lot of people and vehicles on the sand, and bring water and sun protection.

The ferry on July 4th weekend

Holiday weekends are the busiest times for the free Galveston–Port Bolivar ferry, and TxDOT says waits can be two hours or more in the high-traffic season. Cross early in the day or earlier in the week if you can, or come inland by way of I-10 and Highway 124 to avoid the boat. Our Bolivar ferry guide for RVers has the size limits and tips.

Staying at The Palms over the holiday

  • Rate and booking. The July 4th holiday rate is $85 a night. Early reservations are recommended. Prepaid amounts are non-refundable unless management agrees in writing, including for weather and ferry delays.
  • Group size. Each site is limited to 2 adults and 4 children, and extra tents need written approval. For a bigger family, the 3 bed, 2 bath beach house is on Airbnb.
  • Quiet hours. They run 10:30 p.m. to 6:00 a.m. Children must be on their own site during those hours.
  • Pier. The fishing and crabbing pier is for registered guests, with no glass and an adult present for anyone 17 or younger. Outside visitors need management approval.
  • Visitors. Unauthorized visitors aren't allowed, so ask the office before inviting friends over.

Summer heat and storm season

July on the Gulf Coast is hot and humid, so plan for shade, water and a cool RV. It's also hurricane season, which runs June 1 through November 30. Check the forecast before you travel and keep an eye on it during your stay, and read our hurricane season guide for how RVers plan. Frequently asked questions

Are there fireworks in Crystal Beach on the Fourth of July?

Yes. The Bolivar Peninsula Tourism and Visitors Center describes the peninsula's beachfront as a giant fireworks display on July 4th. Check its event listings and CrystalBeach.com for current-year activities.

Can I set off fireworks at The Palms?

No. Fireworks are prohibited at The Palms. Watch the show from the beach instead.

Can I drink alcohol on the beach on July 4th?

According to Galveston County, adults of legal drinking age may drink on Bolivar beaches, but glass containers are not allowed. The beach counts as a road, so no open containers in vehicles and no drinking and driving.

How much is an RV site at The Palms for July 4th week?

The holiday rate is $85 per night, from two days before July 4th through July 4th. Call 936-666-3423 to confirm availability.

How long is the ferry wait on July 4th weekend?

It varies. TxDOT says waits can be two hours or more in the high-traffic season, which includes holiday weekends. You can avoid the ferry by driving inland by way of I-10 and Highway 124.
