RV Lot vs. Apartment in Galveston County: The Real Monthly Cost

By The Palms at Crystal Beach RV Park · November 14, 2026 · 6 min read

An average one-bedroom apartment in Galveston rents for about $1,100 a month before utilities. A premium monthly RV lot at The Palms is $450 with electricity (up to $125) and Wi-Fi included. Here's the full side-by-side comparison, including the costs that come with owning an RV.

If you're deciding between renting an apartment in Galveston County and living in an RV, the monthly rent is only part of the story. Electricity and internet add up quickly. Below we compare an average one-bedroom apartment in Galveston with a monthly RV lot at The Palms at Crystal Beach RV Park, using the most recent public data we could find.

Move-in costs: lease vs. RV lot

The upfront cost gap can be just as large as the monthly one:

  • Apartment: application fees, a security deposit that is often close to a month's rent, possibly pet deposits, and usually a 12-month lease with penalties for breaking it early. You may also need to set up and pay deposits for electric and internet service.
  • The Palms: a $175 security deposit for stays of one week or longer. Electricity (up to $125/month) and Wi-Fi are already set up when you arrive. Monthly rates apply, with discounts if you commit to 6 or 12 months.

Costs that only RV living has

To keep this comparison fair, here's what an apartment renter doesn't pay for:

  • The RV itself: a loan payment if you financed it, plus insurance and registration.
  • Maintenance: roof sealing, A/C service, tires and general upkeep.
  • Propane for cooking, water heating or furnace use.
  • Electric over $125 in a month. This is most likely during peak summer A/C season.
  • Extra vehicles at $30/month each on monthly stays.

If you already own your RV outright, the savings are biggest. If you'd need to buy and finance one, run the numbers on the payment first. For many people it still works out, especially over a few years.

What you get that an apartment doesn't

  • A coastal setting a short drive from the beach in Crystal Beach
  • Fishing and crabbing from an onsite pier
  • Outdoor space at your own site instead of shared hallways
  • The freedom to move your home if your job or plans change
  • A smaller, simpler space to keep up

When an apartment might make more sense

RV living isn't for everyone. If you need more space for a larger family, don't want to maintain a vehicle as your home, or need to live within walking distance of work in Galveston, an apartment may fit better. But if you're a retiree, a traveling worker, a remote worker or anyone who wants to cut monthly costs and live near the beach, an affordable monthly RV lot with electric included is hard to beat.

Frequently asked questions

Is living in an RV park cheaper than renting an apartment in Galveston?

For most people, yes. A one-bedroom apartment in Galveston averages about $1,100 a month before utilities, while a premium monthly RV lot at The Palms is $450 with up to $125 of electricity and free Wi-Fi included. That is roughly $800 or more a month in savings before RV ownership costs like insurance and maintenance.

What does a monthly RV lot cost in Crystal Beach, Texas?

At The Palms, monthly RV lots are $415 for a standard pad and $450 for a premium concrete pad, with electricity included up to $125 a month and free Wi-Fi.

What extra costs come with living in an RV?

Plan for your RV's loan payment if you financed it, insurance, registration, maintenance, propane, any electric usage over the $125 monthly allowance, and $30 a month for each additional vehicle.

What are the move-in costs for a monthly RV site?

The Palms collects a $175 security deposit for stays of one week or longer. Electricity and Wi-Fi are already set up when you arrive, so there are no utility account setups or deposits.
