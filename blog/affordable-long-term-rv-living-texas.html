Affordable Long-Term RV Living in Texas: What $450 a Month Gets You

By The Palms at Crystal Beach RV Park · November 14, 2026 · 6 min read

Long-term RV living can be one of the most affordable ways to live on the Texas coast, if you choose the right park. Here's exactly what a monthly RV lot at The Palms includes, what it doesn't, and who it works best for.

Rent keeps going up, and more people are asking a reasonable question: could I live in an RV full time and actually save money? For many retirees, traveling workers, remote workers and people between homes, the answer is yes, as long as the RV park's monthly rate is fair and you aren't surprised by extra bills.

Here's what affordable long-term RV living looks like at The Palms at Crystal Beach RV Park on Bolivar Peninsula, Texas, including the costs that aren't covered.

Your monthly rent is all bills paid. Water, sewer, trash and internet are included, and electricity is covered up to $125 a month. Those are some of the biggest bills after rent, which is why an RV park with utilities included is so much easier to budget than an apartment. For a line-by-line comparison, see RV lot vs. apartment: real monthly costs in Galveston County.

The costs that aren't included

Honest budgeting means counting everything. Long-term RV living is affordable, but it isn't free:

  • Electric over $125 in a month. Texas summers are hot, and running two air conditioners around the clock in August can push you past the allowance. Shade, reflective window covers and a well-maintained A/C make a real difference.
  • A security deposit. We collect a $175 deposit for stays of one week or longer.
  • Extra vehicles. Additional vehicles are $30/month each on monthly stays.
  • Your RV itself: any loan payment, insurance, registration, routine maintenance and propane.

Even with those costs, many of our monthly guests spend far less each month than they did renting an apartment or house.

Who long-term RV living works well for

Retirees on a fixed income

A predictable monthly rate with electricity and internet built in makes a Social Security or pension budget go much further. Many retirees start as winter snowbirds and end up staying longer.

Traveling and contract workers

If your job brings you to the upper Texas coast for a few months at a time, a monthly RV site is often cheaper and more flexible than a short-term lease or a hotel, and you come home to your own bed every night. See our guide for traveling workers.

Remote workers

Free high-speed Wi-Fi and a quiet, gated park make it possible to work from your rig and walk to the water when you log off.

People in between homes

Building a house, saving for a down payment or figuring out your next move? A monthly RV lot can be a low-cost place to land without signing a long apartment lease.

Why Texas works well for full-time RVers

  • No state income tax. Texas is one of a handful of states without a personal income tax, one reason it's a popular home base for full-time RVers.
  • A mild climate most of the year. The Gulf Coast rarely sees the long, hard winters that make full-time RVing tough up north.
  • Coastal living for less. Living a short drive from the beach usually costs a lot. In an RV park it doesn't have to.

Tips for making long-term RV living affordable

  1. Commit for 6 or 12 months if you can. Ask us about the current discounts.
  2. Watch your electric usage in the hottest and coldest months so you stay under the $125 allowance.
  3. Choose the right pad. Standard pads save $35 a month. Premium concrete pads are cleaner, level and easier to maintain.
  4. Use the amenities. Onsite laundry and free Wi-Fi mean fewer trips to the laundromat and no internet bill, and with water, sewer and trash included there are no utility accounts to set up.
  5. Have a storm plan. Atlantic hurricane season runs June 1 through November 30. One advantage of RV living is that your home can move. Keep your rig road-ready and know your evacuation route during the season.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to live in an RV park long term in Crystal Beach, Texas?

At The Palms, monthly RV lot rent is $415 for a standard pad or $450 for a premium concrete pad. Rent is all bills paid: water, sewer, trash, free high-speed Wi-Fi, and up to $125 of electricity each month. Discounts are available for 6-month and 12-month stays.

Are utilities included in monthly RV lot rent?

Yes. Water, sewer, trash, and free Wi-Fi are included, and electricity is included up to $125 of usage per month. If your electric usage goes over $125 in a month, only the overage is billed.

Is the park an all bills paid RV park?

Yes. Monthly rent is all bills paid: your site, water, sewer, trash, free Wi-Fi, and up to $125 a month of electricity are included. Only electric usage above the $125 allowance and additional vehicles ($30/month each) are billed separately.

Can I live full time at The Palms?

Yes. The Palms offers monthly, long-term RV lot rentals for full-time RVers, retirees, traveling workers and remote workers, with discounts for 6-month and 12-month stays.

Is there a deposit for monthly RV sites?

Yes. There is a $175 security deposit for stays of one week or longer.
