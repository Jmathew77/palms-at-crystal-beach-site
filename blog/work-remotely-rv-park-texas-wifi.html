Remote Work from the Beach: Working from an RV at Crystal Beach

By The Palms at Crystal Beach RV Park · November 14, 2026 · 5 min read

Can you really work from an RV on the Texas coast? Here's what the Wi-Fi, power and workspace look like at The Palms, the backup plan we recommend, and the park rules to know before you book.

More people than ever work from wherever their laptop is, and for many of them that means an RV. If you're thinking about remote work from the Texas Gulf Coast, The Palms at Crystal Beach RV Park offers free high-speed Wi-Fi, a gated park, and monthly lots from $450 with all bills paid. Here is what that setup looks like in practice, including the limits to plan around.

What's included for remote workers

  • Free high-speed Wi-Fi for RV guests
  • All bills paid on monthly stays: water, sewer, trash, and up to $125 of electricity each month
  • Onsite laundry and outdoor showers
  • Gated access and a covered community area
  • Discounts for 6-month and 12-month stays

Plan for the Wi-Fi honestly

Park Wi-Fi is shared among guests, and our rules describe Wi-Fi and amenities as conveniences that are not guaranteed. For email, documents and routine browsing that is usually plenty. If a dropped call would cost you money, treat the park Wi-Fi as your backup rather than your only connection.

  • Bring a backup. A cellular hotspot, or a phone plan with generous tethering, gives you a second way online. If you can, test more than one carrier at your site.
  • Try before you commit. A weekly stay is $200. Spend a week doing your real work on the real connection before you sign up for a month or more.
  • Protect your equipment. A small battery backup (UPS) for your router and modem keeps you online through quick power flickers.
  • Ask before installing anything outside. Our rules don't allow unapproved structures, so check with the office before putting up a satellite dish or antenna.

Power: what to know

Laptops and monitors use very little electricity. The big draw in an RV is the air conditioner, especially in a Texas summer. Monthly rent covers up to $125 of electricity, and usage above that is billed to your account. A few park rules also affect remote workers:

  • A properly rated surge protector is required, and it protects your electronics too.
  • Don't overload the electrical pedestal or use unsafe adapters.
  • Generators are prohibited except in emergencies, so a battery backup is the better plan for outages.

Setting up your workspace

  • Work inside when it's hot. Tents, tarps and unapproved structures aren't allowed on sites, so your best office is inside your RV with the A/C on and a good chair. Many sites have a picnic table for a change of scenery.
  • Mind quiet hours. They run 10:30 p.m. to 6:00 a.m. If you take early or late calls with other time zones, keep them inside and use a headset.
  • Use the community area when you want a different view.

The upside: the water is close

This is why people choose Crystal Beach over a desk in the suburbs. Take a lunch break at the fishing and crabbing pier (it's for registered guests), drive to the beach after your last meeting, or take the free ferry to Galveston on a weekend. Good to know before you book

  • A license, not a lease. Stays are under a site use license agreement, and the RV site can't be used as a permanent address.
  • Mail and packages. They can't be received at the park without prior written management approval, so use a mail-forwarding service or ask the office before shipping anything.
  • RV age. RVs should generally be 10 years old or newer unless management approves an exception.
  • Payments. Weekly and monthly payments are due by 12:00 noon on the renewal date. Tell us before noon that day if you don't plan to renew.
  • Deposit. A $175 security deposit applies to stays of one week or longer.

Frequently asked questions

Is Wi-Fi included at The Palms?

Yes. Free high-speed Wi-Fi is available for RV guests, and it is included with monthly rent.

Is the Wi-Fi good enough for video calls?

It is shared among guests and not guaranteed, so we recommend a cellular hotspot as a backup if your work depends on a steady connection. A one-week stay at $200 is a good way to test it with your real workload.

Is electricity included for remote workers?

Monthly rent includes up to $125 of electricity each month, plus water, sewer and trash. Usage over $125 is billed as an overage, and air conditioning is the biggest factor.

Can I use a satellite dish or a generator?

Generators are prohibited except in emergencies, and unapproved structures are not allowed. Ask the office before installing a satellite dish or antenna.

Can I receive packages at The Palms?

Mail and packages can't be received at the park without prior written management approval. Use a mail-forwarding service or ask the office first.
