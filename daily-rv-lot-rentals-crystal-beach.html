FAQ

Common questions

How much is a daily or nightly RV site?

Daily (nightly) RV lot rentals start at $50 per night for a standard non-concrete pad and $60 per night for a premium concrete pad. Holiday and event rates may apply.

Do I need a reservation for a one-night stay?

Reservations are recommended, especially for summer weekends and events. Check availability online or call 936-666-3423.

Are there holiday or event rates?

Yes. Holiday and event pricing (for example $85/night during July 4th week and Jeep Week) applies on high-demand dates.

What is included with a nightly RV site?

Nightly sites include free high-speed Wi-Fi, gated access, onsite laundry, outdoor showers, and the fishing and crabbing pier.
