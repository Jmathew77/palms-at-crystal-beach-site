FAQ

Quick answers before you book.

Do you offer monthly RV sites in Crystal Beach?

Yes. The Palms offers monthly RV sites, including premium concrete pads and standard non-concrete pads. Current monthly rates start at $420 for standard non-concrete pads and $450 for premium concrete pads. Ask about discounts for 6-month and 12-month stays.

Do you have nightly RV sites?

Yes. Nightly RV sites are available, with standard non-concrete pads starting at $50 per night and premium concrete pads starting at $60 per night. Holiday and event rates may apply.

How many RV sites are available?

The resort has 37 total RV sites: 29 premium concrete pads and 8 standard non-concrete pads.

Is the resort family friendly?

Yes. The Palms is a family-friendly RV resort with a community area, beach access nearby, and guest amenities for short and longer stays.

Is Wi-Fi included?

Yes. Free high-speed Wi-Fi is available for RV guests.

Do you have laundry onsite?

Yes. Washers and dryers are available onsite for weekend guests and monthly guests.

Do you allow long-term RV stays?

Yes. The Palms offers monthly RV site options for guests looking for a longer stay near Crystal Beach and Bolivar Peninsula, with discounts available for 6-month and 12-month stays.

Do you have a beach house rental?

Yes. The Palms offers a 3 bed, 2 bath beach house rental for families, groups, and guests needing extra space.
