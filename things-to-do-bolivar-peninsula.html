Things to Do on Bolivar Peninsula, Texas

By The Palms at Crystal Beach RV Park · Updated November 22, 2026 · 9 min read

Beaches you can drive on, a free ferry, fishing and crabbing, world-class birding, and a year-round calendar of festivals, from the Texas Crab Festival to Jeep Weekend and July 4th beachfront fireworks. Here is your guide to Crystal Beach and the rest of Bolivar Peninsula.

Bolivar Peninsula is a 27-mile strip of Gulf Coast between Galveston Bay and the Gulf of Mexico, reached by the free ferry from Galveston or by road from the east. Crystal Beach sits at its heart, and The Palms at Crystal Beach RV Park is a short drive from the sand. Below you'll find an events calendar, the best outdoor stops, what to eat, and where to stay. We are not affiliated with the events or businesses listed, and dates and hours change, so confirm before you go.

Featured events

Texas Crab Festival (May 6–8, 2027)

The Texas Crab Festival is Crystal Beach's signature spring party. The official site lists the 2027 festival for May 6–8 at Swedes Park and advertises live music all weekend, a two-step dance contest, wiener dog races, a washers tournament, a crab gumbo cook-off, a 5K fun run, a family fun zone, more than 90 booths, and a live auction on Saturday night. It is run by Bolivar Peninsula Charities, a nonprofit. If you plan to attend, reserve your RV site early. See the official Texas Crab Festival site for the current schedule and tickets.

Jeep Weekend (mid-May)

Jeep Weekend, also known as Go Topless Weekend, draws Jeep owners from across Texas and beyond, and it is one of the busiest and most closely policed weekends on the peninsula. In 2026 it ran May 14–18, and Galveston County prohibited camping on the beaches during those dates, so an RV park is the practical place to stay. Dates for 2027 had not been announced as of November 2026. Read our full Jeep Weekend RV camping guide for the rules, permits and traffic tips.

Fourth of July beachfront fireworks

The Bolivar Peninsula Tourism and Visitors Center promotes the Fourth of July here as 27 miles of beachfront fireworks, and crowds arrive for the whole holiday week. Expect heavy traffic on Highway 87 and long ferry waits. Our July 4th guide covers what to plan for, and the holiday rate at The Palms applies from two days before July 4th through July 4th.

Mardi Gras in Crystal Beach (Feb. 6, 2027)

In late winter the peninsula holds its own Mardi Gras parade. The 2027 parade is listed for Saturday, February 6 at 11:30 a.m., traveling from Camp Margaritaville RV Resort along Highway 87 to Gregory Park. It's a good reason to visit in the off-season.

Beaches and outdoors

Crystal Beach and the Bolivar Beach Pavilion

Crystal Beach's long stretch of open sand is the main attraction. The Bolivar Beach Pavilion offers parking, restrooms, rinse-off showers and water fountains. You can drive on the beach without a permit, but a vehicle parked on the sand needs a Bolivar Beach Parking Permit. Rules can tighten during big events, so check before you go.

Gregory Park

Gregory Park in Crystal Beach, at 900 Barbados Dr., has a baseball field and a covered basketball court, and it's where the local Mardi Gras parade ends.

Our own pier and picnic areas

Guests at The Palms can fish and crab from the park's own pier, rinse off at the outdoor showers, and relax in the community area. See our amenities.

History and sights

Fort Travis Seashore Park

In Port Bolivar, near the ferry landing, this park is built around a coastal-artillery fort from the late 1890s. You can explore concrete gun emplacements and bunkers, and the park has shaded picnic areas, wetland overlooks and a playground.

Bolivar Point Lighthouse

Built in 1872 and decommissioned in 1933, the lighthouse is a popular photo stop near Fort Travis. Visitors can't go inside, and it has been repainted in its original black-and-white stripes.

Birding on Bolivar Peninsula

The peninsula sits on a major Gulf Coast flyway, and birders come for shorebirds and, in spring, songbirds. The Bolivar Flats Shorebird Sanctuary is one of the best shorebird sites on the Gulf Coast. At High Island, the Smith Oaks and Boy Scout Woods sanctuaries are known for spring migration. Texas Parks and Wildlife's Bolivar Loop lists more stops, and the free Bolivar Beach Rambles walk is held on the first Saturday of each month.

Fishing and crabbing

Surf, bay and pier fishing are all part of life here, and crabbing is popular too. Our fishing and crabbing guide covers what you can catch and the license and crab rules to check before you set a line or trap. Fishing fans can also plan around the Saltwater Slam tournament in October.

Food and live music

Crystal Beach has a lively mix of waterfront seafood, beach bars and icehouses, many with live music on warm-weather weekends. Stingaree Restaurant & Marina, the Nauti Beaver Hut, Tiki Beach Bar & Grill and HardHeads Icehouse & Grill come up most often in local guides. Our things to do and where to eat guide has a full table with where each place is and what it's known for. Hours change, so call ahead.

Day trips: the free ferry to Galveston

The Galveston-Bolivar ferry is free, runs 24 hours a day, and has been operated by TxDOT since 1934. The crossing takes about 18 minutes, and on the other side you'll find the Strand district, museums and Galveston Island beaches. Expect waits on summer and holiday weekends, and read our Bolivar ferry guide if you're towing an RV.

The best time to visit

  • Spring (March–May): Spring break, songbird migration, the Texas Crab Festival and Jeep Weekend. Busy and lively.
  • Summer (June–August): The warmest beach weather, July 4th fireworks, and sailboat and golf cart events. Book early.
  • Fall (September–November): Cook-offs, the Saltwater Slam, Halloween events and the Christmas Market, with fewer crowds and cooler days.
  • Winter (December–February): Quiet beaches, mild Gulf Coast weather, New Year's Eve fireworks and Mardi Gras. A popular season for snowbirds and monthly stays; see our snowbird guide.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best things to do on Bolivar Peninsula?

Popular choices are the open Gulf beach at Crystal Beach, Fort Travis and the Bolivar Point Lighthouse, birding at Bolivar Flats and High Island, fishing and crabbing, the free Galveston-Bolivar ferry, and annual events such as the Texas Crab Festival, Jeep Weekend and July 4th beachfront fireworks.

When is the Texas Crab Festival in 2027?

The official Texas Crab Festival website lists the 2027 festival as May 6–8 at Swedes Park in Crystal Beach.

When is Jeep Weekend in Crystal Beach?

Jeep Weekend is held in mid-May. It ran May 14–18 in 2026 and May 16–19 in 2025. Dates for 2027 had not been announced as of November 2026, so check Galveston County and the Bolivar Peninsula Tourism and Visitors Center for updates.

Are there fireworks on Bolivar Peninsula on the Fourth of July?

Yes. The Bolivar Peninsula Tourism and Visitors Center promotes the Fourth of July as 27 miles of beachfront fireworks along the peninsula.

Is the Galveston-Bolivar ferry free?

Yes. The TxDOT ferry between Port Bolivar and Galveston is free and runs 24 hours a day. Waits can be long on busy weekends and holidays.

Can I drive on the beach in Crystal Beach?

You can drive on the beach without a permit, but a vehicle parked on the sand needs a Bolivar Beach Parking Permit. Rules can change for events, so confirm with Galveston County before you go.
